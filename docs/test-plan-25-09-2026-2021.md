# Telablur test plan — status and next steps

Everything below is reconstructible from disk: every test set writes its own
`manifest.json` (job IDs, params, output paths) into its `out/NN_name/`
folder, and every script is standalone. Nothing here depends on conversation
history.

## What's been tested (01–08)

| # | Folder | Question | Finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | `01_probe/` | Does the pipeline work end to end? | Yes — submit → poll → fetch works. |
| 02 | `02_recursion/` | What happens feeding output back as input, 5x? | Chain degrades toward noise without a mask. |
| 03 | `03_masked_pattern/` | Can a mask localise the effect on a simple pattern? | Yes, mask works as expected (white=effect, black=protected). |
| 04 | `04_person_mask/` | Can rembg auto-segment a person and drive Telablur's mask? | Yes. Effect-on-background (person protected) is the strongest result — subject stays pixel-sharp, background fully dissolves. |
| 05 | `05_person_mask_recursion/` | Does a protected subject survive 5 rounds of background-only recursion? | Yes — person stayed pixel-perfect across all 5 states; background cycled through real content without turning to mud. |
| 06 | `06_param_sweep/` | One-variable-at-a-time sweep (strength, direction, size, downscale, mask) on a fixed real image pair. | Strength doesn't reduce blockiness, just lets original colour bleed through. Direction is the real lever for noise *character*. |
| 07 | `07_self_test/` | Is the grid/blockiness noise from blending two different photos, or inherent to the encode/measure step? | **Inherent.** Same image in both slots still shows the same grid — it's an encoding-floor artifact, not a content-blend artifact. |
| 08 | `08_direction_strength_grid/` | Wide-open strength (0.1–0.9) × direction (full/vertical/horizontal) grid — no presupposed "winner" — run both self-case and two-image+mask case. | **Just completed (30/30 jobs).** Results not yet reviewed — next action is to look at the two `self/` and `two_images/` output sets side by side. |

Three composable levers are now established:
- **Mask** — spatial control (where the effect applies)
- **Direction** — noise character (full = grid/crosshatch; vertical/horizontal = streakier, sometimes more recognisable)
- **Strength** — how much of image2's content shows through (independent of noise character)
